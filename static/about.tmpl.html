    Kenton Vizdos

    Full-stack engineer in Richmond, VA, turning messy real-world processes into software people can actually use.

    I focus on building accessible, secure, and practical full-stack applications, mostly in Go and TypeScript. As technical co-founder of Edvizion, I build and run software for K–12 schools. That work has included ecommerce, enrollment systems, workflow automation, reporting, integrations, and occasionally sending faxes with code.

    Outside of work, I spend a lot of time experimenting with Go, distributed systems, product design, security, home automation, and whatever else has caught my attention that week.

    Experience

    1. Edvizion

      June 2023 – Present · Remote

      Technical Co-founder & Software Engineer

      I architect and maintain cloud-based education systems in Go, TypeScript, and AWS, with event-driven integrations, for three schools: two public schools in one district and a private school. They cover student enrollment, attendance, reporting, and ecommerce. My work focuses on replacing manual processes with reliable systems, improving operational visibility, and making complicated workflows easier for schools and families.

      Records requests used to mean staff taking printers home and working late into the night every peak season. After I automated them, one staff member told us, in the middle of the next peak, that it "works great for when we don't have a lot going on." They hadn't noticed it was their busiest time of the year. I also redesigned attendance reporting for an online school, increasing average documented instructional time by about 7 hours per student per week, and built enrollment tracking and analytics across multiple systems.

      With Michael Vizdos, I also co-lead Academic Threat Intelligence, an applied research initiative mapping the gray-market ecosystem and incentives behind academic fraud in K–12 and higher-ed distance learning. It's the basis for my talks, including the MCTA keynote.
