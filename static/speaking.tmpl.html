    Speaking

    I talk with educators, testing professionals, and distance-learning leaders about the economics behind academic integrity.

    My talks come out of Academic Threat Intelligence, the applied research Michael Vizdos and I lead at Edvizion, mapping the gray-market ecosystem and incentives behind academic fraud in K–12 and higher-ed distance learning.

    Talk

    The Integrity Illusion Mapping the Ecosystem of Buyers, Sellers, and Incentives

    Kenton Vizdos & Michael Vizdos · Edvizion

    Most conversations about academic integrity focus on individual student behavior and traditional "cheating catchers." This session zooms out to the whole economic ecosystem driving those actions: gray-market "homework helpers," misaligned EdTech vendor incentives, and institutional pressure focused on graduation and retention rates.

    We explore the tension between EdTech platforms driven by usage metrics and institutions driven by outcomes, shifting the conversation from a localized policing problem to a systemic market challenge. Through scenario-based discussion, attendees examine their own institutional leverage and the question at the center of the talk.

    “ If you had exact intelligence on who is buying and selling your institution's integrity, what could you actually do about it?
