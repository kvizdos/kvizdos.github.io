Hi, I'm Kenton Vizdos.

I'm a full-stack engineer in Richmond, VA, turning messy real-world processes into software people can actually use. Right now that means internal tools and student-facing systems for K–12 education, mostly in Go, TypeScript, and Web Components. I also co-founded Edvizion, and this year gave the MCTA keynote and spoke at USDLA about the economics of academic integrity in The Integrity Illusion.
