The Integrity Illusion: Mapping the Ecosystem of Buyers, Sellers, and Incentives
Kenton Vizdos
Edvizion 
Michael Vizdos 
Edvizion 
Most conversations about academic integrity focus on individual student behavior and traditional "cheating catchers." However, what happens when we zoom out and examine the entire economic ecosystem driving these actions? Behind the scenes of distance learning, there is a complex web of gray-market "homework helpers," misaligned EdTech vendor incentives, and institutional pressures focused heavily on graduation and retention rates.
 
In this session, Edvizion founders Kenton and Michael Vizdos will report on the findings from Monday night’s live "Ecosystem Mapping" event.  Participants will explore the tension between EdTech platforms driven by usage metrics and institutions driven by outcomes, shifting the conversation from a localized policing problem to a systemic market challenge. Through interactive, scenario-based discussion, attendees will be challenged to confront the realities of this convoluted market. Participants will examine their own institutional leverage and address a difficult question: If you had exact intelligence on who is buying and selling your institution's integrity, what could you actually do about it?
 
Join us to move beyond treating the symptoms and start building a coalition to address the disease.
